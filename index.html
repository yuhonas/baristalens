Specialty Coffee Barista Rubric

Score your pour across the 5 international championship criteria (0 to 10 scale).

1. Bilateral Symmetry & Pattern Balance

Alignment of left and right wing structures relative to central pour axis.

2. Contrast & Crema Definition

Crisp contrast boundary between glossy white microfoam and dark hazelnut crema.

3. Stem Cut-Through & Axis Alignment

Linear path of final pull-through slice through heart center without hooking.

4. Microfoam Gloss & Aeration Quality

Silky wet-paint sheen with zero visible macro bubbles or dry foam clumps.

5. Cup Placement & Size Harmony

Pattern fills 70–85% of cup surface, oriented cleanly toward mug handle.
